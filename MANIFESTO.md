# BioCipher Manifesto 🧬✨

Life is information.

DNA is **source code**.  
Transcription is **compilation**.  
Translation is **execution**.  
Mass spectra are **runtime traces**.

BioCipher exists to model biological flow as a composable pipeline:
Sequence → Protein → Signal → Inference.

This repo is intentionally modular:
- **Sequence Engine** (Central Dogma)
- **Proteomics Engine** (Mass-spectrum inference)
- **Pipeline Layer** (end-to-end demos)

Vision: keep it simple, make it extensible, and keep it beautiful.
